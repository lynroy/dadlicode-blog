Cybersecurity for Caribbean Small Businesses

Simple habits that help protect customer records, devices, and business operations.

Small businesses across the Caribbean increasingly depend on email, online payments, cloud storage, and mobile devices. That convenience also creates risks when accounts are poorly protected or important information is not backed up.

A sensible starting point is to use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication, keep software updated, restrict access to sensitive records, and make regular backups. Staff should know how to recognize suspicious messages and how to report mistakes quickly.

Cybersecurity planning also includes recovery. Businesses should know who to contact after an incident, what systems are essential, and how they would continue serving customers if an account or computer becomes unavailable.

The goal is not to make every small business an expert in cybersecurity. It is to adopt manageable controls that fit the business and improve them over time.
