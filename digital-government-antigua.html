Digital Government in Antigua and Barbuda

How reliable online public services can reduce paperwork, improve access, and build public confidence.

Digital public services have the potential to make everyday transactions easier for residents and businesses in Antigua and Barbuda. Rather than travelling between offices for routine requests, people could complete more steps online, receive updates, and keep a record of their submissions.

The challenge is not simply putting a form on a website. A useful service must have clear instructions, accessible design, secure identity checks where necessary, and a reliable process behind the screen. Agencies also need to consider people with limited connectivity or digital skills.

For small island states, a practical approach is to begin with a few high-demand services, measure whether they save time, and improve them based on user feedback. Integration between departments can help reduce repeated data entry, but privacy, governance, and accountability must be built into the design.

At DadliCode, we are interested in building technology that addresses real operational needs. The most successful digital transformation is not the flashiest website; it is a service that people can trust and use.
